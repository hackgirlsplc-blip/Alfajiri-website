What the name means.

Alfajiri is the Swahili word for dawn; the hour before sunrise, when the work is already underway. That's the standard we build to: systems that are ready and running long before anyone's watching.

We're a team of engineers, analysts and architects working across software, data and security. No handoffs between disciplines; the people who design your data pipeline are the ones who secure it, and the ones who ship your product are the ones who keep it running.

"We are the alpha" isn't a slogan about being first to market. It's about being first to understand the problem properly.
